# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, technical leaders, researchers, and potential collaborators evaluating Pallavi Raiturkar for senior engineering roles, AI product leadership, research-informed product work, or collaboration.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio gives visitors concrete evidence of Pallavi's work across AI systems, developer tools, full-stack engineering, and human-centered research. It should make three things clear:

1. Pallavi is a staff software engineer building GitHub Copilot and leading technically complex AI product work.
2. Her research background is not a decorative origin story; it taught her to treat user behavior as evidence and to keep what people need at the center of development.
3. She maintains a separate personal studio practice that explores form and medium without competing with her engineering identity.

Success means a visitor can identify Pallavi's current scope, review selected outcomes, open her publications, understand how research shapes her engineering judgment, and contact her through a verified public channel.

## Brand Personality

Precise, substantive, curious, and quietly expressive. The site should feel like a senior engineer's working portfolio with an independent studio sensibility: highly polished, direct about the work, and willing to shift visual language when the content changes.

## Anti-references

- Abstract storytelling that makes the visitor decode Pallavi's career.
- Orange-and-white startup landing pages.
- Generic portfolio templates and repeated card grids.
- Neon or cyberpunk developer aesthetics.
- Stiff, document-like resume pages.
- Art-first ambiguity that obscures Pallavi's engineering work.
- Generic AI-generated landing-page tropes, including gradient text, glassmorphism, repeated section eyebrows, numbered section scaffolding, and cream or beige backgrounds.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence before narrative.** Lead with roles, systems, outcomes, and publications; let the career throughline emerge from the work.
2. **User empathy is an engineering practice.** Connect HCI research to how Pallavi measures behavior, evaluates quality, and changes products in response.
3. **Give each track its own room.** Work, research, and studio practice should feel related but never collapsed into one metaphor.
4. **Make depth scannable.** Senior technical scope must be understandable quickly, with enough detail for an interested visitor to keep reading.
5. **Use visual expression where it belongs.** The engineering portfolio stays exact and structured; the studio section earns a more image-led composition.
6. **End in a real conversation.** Contact paths use only verified email and public profiles.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for color contrast and interaction. Use semantic HTML, logical heading structure, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus states, 44-by-44-pixel minimum touch targets, and text that remains usable at 200% zoom. All meaningful motion must have a reduced-motion alternative. Images require useful alt text, and publication links must make their destination clear.
